# HR-Analytics-PowerBI
Interactive HR Analytics Dashboard built using Power BI to analyze employee attrition and workforce trends.


Project Overview

This project is an interactive HR Analytics Dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI.

The objective of this dashboard is to analyze employee attrition and identify key workforce patterns across different employee demographics and job-related factors.

---
Business Objective

- Monitor overall employee attrition
- Analyze attrition across different age groups
- Identify high-attrition salary segments
- Analyze attrition by gender
- Understand attrition across education backgrounds
- Identify job roles with high employee turnover
- Analyze attrition across job levels

Key Insights

### Age

Employees aged 26–35 represent the largest attrition group, with 116 employees leaving.

This group contributes approximately 49% of total attrition.

### Salary

Employees earning below 5K represent the largest attrition segment.

Approximately 77% of total attrition comes from this salary category.

### Gender

Male employees account for 140 attritions, compared with 79 female attritions.

However, attrition rate should be compared by gender to account for differences in the underlying employee population.

### Education

Life Sciences represents the largest education category among employees who left, followed by Medical and Marketing.

### Job Role

Laboratory Technicians, Sales Executives and Research Scientists are among the job roles with the highest attrition.

## Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

1. Investigate retention challenges among employees aged 26–35.
2. Analyze compensation and career progression for lower-paid employees.
3. Investigate high-attrition job roles.
4. Analyze employee satisfaction, overtime and promotion history.
5. Compare attrition rates rather than only absolute attrition counts.
6. Develop targeted employee retention strategies.
